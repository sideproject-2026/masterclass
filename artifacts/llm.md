# Overview
Design an Learning Management System with two model an Instructor and Student. The LMS will be a service which they can subscribe similar to dometrain.com for engineering level. 

## Minimum Viable Functionality

1. Instructor can create a module and upload a video or make a readable content. The upload initially thru Youtube.

2.  Student can browse all the available learning uploaded by the instructor

3. Instructor can login and access instructor studio where they can setup their modules, chapter and upload video with notes or create a readable content

4. Student can register and enroll to each module.

5. once the user is completed to the module. There will be a congratulation and suggestion. for now as part of MVF we are not going make a certificate.
